These are public protocol facts, not application sample balances.

The test transport serves a deliberately bounded single-transaction scenario. Address counters and UTXO responses model that scenario, not a claim about the address's complete real history or present balance. Failure, retry, archive/restore and recovery exercises use this fixed observation. Synthetic adversarial chain fixtures separately cover reorg, replacement and incomplete scans. This fixture does not establish independent full-wallet completeness or close the broader chain-security release gates.
