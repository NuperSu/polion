# Accounting

The FIFO engine consumes explicit accounting treatments attached to confirmed ledger movements. Every movement must be assigned once; acquired amounts, disposal proceeds, fees and internal transfers are never inferred from transaction shape. Every known treatment value and unit price identifies its quote asset; a report requires an explicit quote asset and rejects mixed quote assets. Opening lots may have unknown basis. Transfers require equal asset amounts and a shared owner; they retain original lot age and proportional basis.

Costs and proceeds are arbitrary precision rational totals. FIFO splits basis proportionally without rounding, and reports retain each consumed lot, missing inventory, fee status, and originating activity. Missing inventory or cost propagates unknown basis and gain. Unpriced positions have unknown value and unrealized gain. Current unit prices are quote currency per atomic unit. Totals and fee policy require explicit caller classification; results are tax-independent and do not claim jurisdictional compliance. Inputs plus decisions can be replayed deterministically to rebuild reports.

Reports exclude an asset from FIFO inventory and disposal results while any confirmed activity for that asset has missing or invalid treatments. This prevents skipped movements from leaving stale lots available to later disposals. The report identifies the activities requiring repair; unrelated assets remain reportable. Legacy treatments without a quote currency are invalid and can be explicitly reclassified through the treatment editor.
