## Execution definitions

These are proposed deterministic execution defaults for later implementation and require user agreement; they are not claims that the calculations exist, have passed an oracle, or have been approved. Exact values and source/time provenance must be retained. Decisions and the crypto-market benchmark also need substantive product choices before their modeled output can be defined.

| Item | Proposed transparent definition / unresolved decision |
| --- | --- |
| Portfolio change | Default to 1D and follow the selected chart range. Show end value less start value and, for a positive start, the difference divided by start; include external flows and label this **value change**, not return. Missing endpoint valuation makes the affected result unavailable. |
| Return | For each subperiod separated by an external contribution or withdrawal, set `r = ending value before external flow / starting value after external flow − 1`. TWR is `product(1 + r) − 1` over the subperiods. At the initial boundary start after flows at that instant; at the final boundary end before flows at that instant. Order coincident flows together. Require valuations at flow boundaries and positive denominators; omit the result if an input is missing. Internal transfers are not flows across the selected scope. Fees paid by accounts in scope reduce portfolio value and are not external withdrawals; fees paid outside scope are excluded and disclosed. Exclude pending, cancelled and reversed events; use the effective version of corrected events. Benchmark series use identical boundaries and quote currency. |
| Price change | `(end − start) / start` for positive start price, over the named rolling window. Use observations at or before each boundary only if they meet the applicable source freshness rule; expose source and observation times. Missing/stale endpoints make change unavailable. |
| Average buy/sell | Sum executed quote consideration and divide by executed base quantity separately for buys and sells in the selected scope. Exclude fees, transfers, cancelled and reversed trades; show fees separately. Missing consideration or quantity makes the affected average incomplete. |
| Total gains | Realized plus unrealized gains, in the same quote currency, scope and valuation time, only when both components have sufficient evidence. Unknown basis or ordering stays unknown/provisional, never zero by default. |
| Decisions | Product definition pending: agree the retrospective comparison, horizon, fee treatment, baseline and uncertainty. Do not display unsupported good/bad verdicts. |
| Exchange usage/location | Rank explicitly recorded venues by count of eligible completed trades, one count per trade; omit unknown venues and show their count separately. Location is the share of priced position value held by explicitly recorded account/location, with unpriced holdings disclosed separately. Never infer exchange or brand from an address. |
| Trade counts and fees | Count eligible executed trades once, excluding reversed/cancelled trades and transfer legs. Attribute each fee to its recorded paying account and event once; display exact native-asset totals. Convert at the fee event timestamp using an eligible quote, with missing conversions shown as incomplete. Corrected events use their effective version. |
| Benchmark | BTC can be compared as a quote-currency price index normalized to 100 at the common start. A crypto-market benchmark and its constituents, weighting and source require product agreement; do not label a local subset as the market. |
| Transaction worth and unit price | Worth Then is the absolute asset quantity in the card multiplied by an eligible unit-price observation at or before the event timestamp; Worth Now uses the same quantity and the eligible observation at or before the evaluation instant. Use the selected portfolio quote currency for both and disclose observation source/time. If either price is unavailable or stale, that worth is unavailable. A unit price is quote currency per one whole asset unit, never position value. Exclude pending, cancelled and reversed events from completed-trade aggregates; retain their status in the timeline and use the effective corrected event. |
| Asset Worth | Show **position value**, quantity × eligible unit price at each timestamp in the selected portfolio and quote currency. Label the chart as value, not return; quantity changes and missing quotes must be visible. |
| Chart ranges | End at the evaluation instant. 1H/1D/1W/1M/3M/1Y mean trailing 1 hour/24 hours/7 days/30 days/90 days/365 days; YTD begins at 00:00 UTC on 1 January; ALL begins at the earliest eligible scoped ledger or holding record for portfolio value, or at the earliest eligible price observation for a market-only price chart. Target cadence: 1 minute for 1H, 30 minutes for 1D, 4 hours for 1W, 1 day for 1M and 3M, and 1 week for YTD, 1Y and ALL. Include start and end boundaries. At each sample use the latest observation at or before the boundary only while it meets its source freshness rule; otherwise leave a gap. Do not interpolate observations or connect across gaps as facts. If a provider cannot supply that cadence or history, disclose its actual coverage and leave unsupported intervals unavailable; do not silently alias a different range. Model Portfolio offers 1H/1D/1W/1M/YTD/1Y/ALL; coin offers 1H/1D/1W/1M/3M/1Y. |

## States and acceptance work

Each component needs distinct loading, empty, offline, stale, error and partial-data behavior appropriate to its evidence. Keep saved results visible through refresh/failure, identify missing observations locally, and do not draw across missing data as if observed. Known zero requires evidence; unknown is not zero. Provide concise metric-specific explanations and source/time details on demand. Empty holdings, market-only assets, no transactions, unavailable feeds, missing basis, missing historical quotes and provisional ordering need explicit fixture and platform coverage.


### Surface states and language

These are proposed presentation contracts. Loading retains eligible saved content with a localized progress indicator; offline and failure retain it with its source, observation/acceptance time and stale status where applicable. An unavailable value is shown as a dash or concise unavailable label, never a fabricated zero. Retry is offered where an operation can be retried; details are available on demand.

| Surface | Empty, loading, offline and error behavior | Wording within the modeled composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main | With no saved market data show an empty message in each affected section; load each feed independently. Offline or a failed feed retains its cache and marks its source/time and staleness; retry that feed. | Name the ranked universe, window and source; call a local subset local, not a global ranking. |
| Portfolio | With no holdings show an empty holdings component and add action; while snapshot/history loads keep saved balance and chart. Offline or history failure retains saved results and exposes retry/details; missing prices leave affected totals or points unavailable. | Label the period and **value change**, including flows, rather than investment return; show quote and valuation time. |
| Coin General | Market-only is a valid empty ownership state, not zero basis. While prices load keep saved price/chart; offline or failure marks source/time/staleness and offers retry; missing history leaves gaps. | Identify the actual provider or venue and pair, not “global average” unless supported. Owned and gains unavailable without evidence are dashes. |
| Coin Transactions | No matching records shows a scoped empty message and add action. Loading retains the last result for the same scope; query failure retains it with retry, and offline uses saved local records. | Show status and provenance; missing counterparties, fee conversions or historical worth are unavailable; averages describe eligible trades and fee treatment. |
| Insights | Empty scope shows empty components; each section loads independently and retains saved scoped results on failure/offline with time/source and retry where applicable. Missing inputs leave only the affected metric unavailable. | Label value versus return, allocation denominator and unpriced coverage; show missing or provisional basis as a dash with explanation. Decisions remains unavailable until its definition is agreed. |
| Accounts | No accounts shows connect; load saved accounts while status refreshes. Offline or failed refresh keeps identities and last accepted time, identifies the affected source and offers retry/details. | Distinguish saved account from accepted refresh; never imply complete history merely from a successful request. |
| Alerts | No rules shows an empty list and add action when supported. Loading retains saved rules; offline or evaluation failure preserves rules and shows last evaluation/source and retry or delivery status. | Until implemented, show unavailable in the intended component; do not imply continuous monitoring. |
| Order book | No selected venue/pair prompts selection when supported. Loading may retain the last snapshot with venue/time; offline, stale sequence or failure marks it stale and offers reconnect. | Name the venue, pair and observation time; never label one venue global. Until supported, show unavailable. |
| Manual entry / import | Empty draft shows field prompts and validation; saving/import loading retains the draft or preview and prevents duplicate submission. Offline local entry remains available; errors retain input and show actionable field/operation details and retry. An empty import preview is explicit. | Identify asset, account, quote, fee and time zone; unsupported trade/import combinations remain unavailable rather than silently reclassified. |
