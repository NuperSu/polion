# Sync orchestration

The SQLite-backed orchestrator schedules one history page per invocation. Jobs persist their source, account, network, cursor, overlap/finality hints, progress, coverage, retries and lease token. Each fetched page and its checkpoint commit in one transaction; failures leave both untouched. Cancellation and expired leases fence stale workers. A completed result requires a provider to explicitly declare complete coverage; partial and unknown remain visible.

Workers claim jobs transactionally, limit concurrent claims and prevent simultaneous jobs for the same source/account/network. Provider cooldown, retry backoff and failure counts are durable. Alternate providers are attempted after backoff; error records contain a fixed category rather than potentially sensitive exception text. Repeated page cursors are rejected. Callers provide a bounded transport timeout, invoke workers on appropriate platform background infrastructure, and supply chain-specific overlap/finality hints. Job recovery reclaims expired leases after process death. Polling, platform background scheduling, provider-specific finality rules and wall-clock management are integration responsibilities.
